Privacy and limitations

Your text stays in your browser

Pasted text and imported files are processed locally by JavaScript and browser workers. This app does not send document contents to a server, use analytics, or store review text in an account or database. Reloading the page clears the current review.

The hosting provider receives ordinary website requests when your browser downloads the app. This is separate from processing your text. Downloaded copies remain on your device; copying puts text on your system clipboard.

Review before sharing

Pattern checks can miss sensitive information and flag harmless examples. No findings is not a guarantee of safety. Choosing Keep does not verify a value is safe. If a real credential was already exposed, redacting a copy does not revoke it.

Supported imports

Text and code files (including .ino): up to 200 KB. PDF and DOCX: up to 5 MB. Extracted text: up to 50,000 text units; PDFs: up to 100 pages. Scanned images require OCR elsewhere. Legacy .doc files must be converted to .docx. Unsupported, encrypted, or incomplete extraction is reported.

Exports contain reviewed plain text. They do not edit the original file or preserve Word/PDF formatting, and do not remove hidden metadata or embedded objects from an original document.
